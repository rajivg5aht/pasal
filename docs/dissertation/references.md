# Verified references

These sources were checked on 14 October 2026. Publisher metadata or author-hosted
material was used. Access dates concern this verification, not publication dates.

- Burke, R. (2002). Hybrid Recommender Systems: Survey and Experiments.
  *User Modeling and User-Adapted Interaction*, 12, 331–370.
  https://doi.org/10.1023/A:1021240730564
  Publisher page: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1023/A:1021240730564
  Verification scope: bibliographic metadata and abstract; subscription full text
  was not accessed. No claim of full-paper inspection is made.
- Sarwar, B., Karypis, G., Konstan, J. and Riedl, J. (2001). Item-Based
  Collaborative Filtering Recommendation Algorithms. *Proceedings of WWW10*.
  Author-group copy: https://files.grouplens.org/papers/www10_sarwar.pdf
- Manning, C. D., Raghavan, P. and Schütze, H. (2008). *Introduction to
  Information Retrieval*. Cambridge University Press.
  Author-hosted text: https://nlp.stanford.edu/IR-book/html/htmledition/irbook.html
  Specific methods: https://nlp.stanford.edu/IR-book/html/htmledition/tf-idf-weighting-1.html
  and https://nlp.stanford.edu/IR-book/html/htmledition/dot-products-1.html
- Ji, Y., Sun, A., Zhang, J. and Li, C. (2023). A Critical Study on Data Leakage
  in Recommender System Offline Evaluation. *ACM Transactions on Information
  Systems*, 41(3), Article 75, 1–27. https://doi.org/10.1145/3569930
  Author manuscript and journal metadata: https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.11060
- Lin, J., Dai, X., Xi, Y., Liu, W., Chen, B., Zhang, H., Liu, Y., Wu, C., Li, X.,
  Zhu, C., Guo, H., Yu, Y., Tang, R. and Zhang, W. (2024). How Can Recommender
  Systems Benefit from Large Language Models: A Survey. *arXiv*, version 6,
  9 July 2024. https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.05817v6
  Version cited explicitly; first arXiv submission was in 2023.
- Retailrocket (n.d.). *Retailrocket recommender system dataset*. Kaggle.
  https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/retailrocket/ecommerce-dataset
  Publisher data card: event counts, temporal properties and anonymisation.
- SciPy contributors (n.d.). *scipy.stats.permutation_test*.
  https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.stats.permutation_test.html
  Reference for within-pair label exchange and paired-difference sign changes.

  https://www.statsmodels.org/stable/_modules/statsmodels/stats/multitest.html
  Reference for Holm step-down Bonferroni adjustment.
